0x01 // Technical Specification & Remediation Patch

### Summary
In `onnx/checker.cc`, `onnx/defs/parser.h`, and `onnx/defs/parser.cc` (`<= 1.21.0`), three defensive validation gaps exist in external tensor path/descriptor verification and text-format `OnnxParser` numeric literal parsing:

### Root Cause Analysis
1. **`#`-Prefixed Path Bypass in `resolve_external_data_location()` and `check_external_data_size()` (`onnx/checker.cc:291-302, 1634-1694`)**:
   - In `resolve_external_data_location()` (`onnx/checker.cc:1661-1693`), after constructing `auto data_path = base_dir_path / relative_path; auto data_path_str = path_to_utf8(data_path);`, the security checks (`verify_path_containment(data_path, base_dir, tensor_name)` at `line 1673`, `!std::filesystem::is_regular_file(data_path)` at `line 1676`, and `std::filesystem::hard_link_count(data_path) > 1` at `line 1685`) are gated behind `if (data_path_str[0] != '#')`.
   - Similarly, `check_external_data_size()` (`onnx/checker.cc:300`) returns early on `if (data_path_str.empty() || data_path_str[0] == '#')`.
   - When `base_dir` is `""` (or itself begins with `#`) and a tensor's `external_data` `location` starts with `#` (such as `#symlink_dir/target` traversing a symlinked parent directory, a `#`-prefixed hardlink, a `#`-prefixed FIFO/directory, or an undersized `#`-prefixed file), `data_path_str[0] == '#'`, causing both `resolve_external_data_location()` and `check_external_data_size()` to skip canonical path containment, regular-file verification, hardlink count verification, and file-size/offset/length bounds checking.
2. **Missing Post-Open Regular-File Verification (`S_ISREG` / `FILE_TYPE_DISK`) in `open_external_data()` (`onnx/checker.cc:1746-1754, 1888-1906`)**:
   - `open_external_data()` re-verifies the opened descriptor/handle after `open()` / `CreateFileW()` to prevent TOCTOU swaps between `resolve_external_data_location()` and file opening.
   - However, on POSIX (`onnx/checker.cc:1889-1906`), `fstat(fd, &fd_stat)` checks inode identity and `fd_stat.st_nlink > 1` without checking `!S_ISREG(fd_stat.st_mode)`. On Windows (`onnx/checker.cc:1747-1753`), `GetFileInformationByHandle(h, &file_info)` checks `file_info.dwFileAttributes & FILE_ATTRIBUTE_REPARSE_POINT` (and `nNumberOfLinks > 1` at `line 1780`) without checking `GetFileType(h) == FILE_TYPE_DISK` or `FILE_ATTRIBUTE_DIRECTORY`.
   - Consequently, a named pipe (`FIFO`) or character/device node swapped in after the pre-open check (or reached via a `#`-prefixed path) passes post-open validation in `open_external_data()`.
3. **Uncaught `std::out_of_range` / `std::invalid_argument` and Negative-to-Unsigned Wrap in `OnnxParser` (`onnx/defs/parser.h:306-350`, `onnx/defs/parser.cc:237-244`)**:
   - `ParserBase::Parse(Literal&)` (`onnx/defs/parser.cc:237-244`) scans optional exponent digits via `while (!AtEnd() && IsDigit(Cur())) ++pos_;` without verifying that at least one digit was consumed, accepting truncated exponent literals (`1e`, `1e+`, `1E-`) as valid `FLOAT_LITERAL` tokens.
   - `ParserBase::Parse(int64_t&)`, `Parse(uint64_t&)`, `Parse(float&)`, and `Parse(double&)` (`onnx/defs/parser.h:306-350`) invoke `std::stoll`, `std::stoull`, `LocaleIndependentStof`, and `LocaleIndependentStod` without `ONNX_TRY` / `ONNX_CATCH`, allowing malformed or out-of-range numeric literals in untrusted `.onnxtxt` inputs to throw uncaught `std::invalid_argument` or `std::out_of_range` exceptions instead of returning `Common::Status` via `ParseError()`. Furthermore, `Parse(uint64_t&)` calls `std::stoull` on negative `INT_LITERAL` tokens (e.g. `-1`), silently wrapping them modulo $2^{64}$ to `UINT64_MAX`.

### Impact
- Bypass of `verify_path_containment()`, regular-file checks, hard-link checks, and `check_external_data_size()` when `data_path_str` begins with `#`; blocking hangs or special-file reads if a `FIFO`/device node is opened in `open_external_data()`; and uncaught C++ standard library exceptions (`std::out_of_range` / `std::invalid_argument`) aborting processes that parse untrusted ONNX text models via `OnnxParser`.

### Recommended Remediation
1. Enforce `verify_path_containment()`, `is_regular_file()`, `hard_link_count()`, and `check_external_data_size()` unconditionally in `onnx/checker.cc` regardless of whether `data_path_str` starts with `'#'`.
2. Verify `S_ISREG(fd_stat.st_mode)` after `fstat()` on POSIX and `GetFileType(h) == FILE_TYPE_DISK` (plus `!FILE_ATTRIBUTE_DIRECTORY`) on Windows inside `open_external_data()`.
3. Require at least one digit after an exponent marker (`pos_ == exp_digits_start`) in `ParserBase::Parse(Literal&)`, reject negative literals in `Parse(uint64_t&)`, and guard `stoll`/`stoull`/`LocaleIndependentStof`/`LocaleIndependentStod` with `ONNX_TRY` / `ONNX_CATCH`:
