0x01 // Technical Specification & Remediation Patch

### Summary
In `src/utils/ssrf-protection.ts` (`<= v2.99.1`), `SSRFProtection.validateUrlSync()` and `SSRFProtection.validateWebhookUrl()` normalize `url.hostname` via `.toLowerCase()` and IPv6 bracket-stripping, without stripping trailing DNS root-zone dots (`.`) and without checking RFC 6761 `.localhost` subdomains in `isLoopbackHost()`.

Because the WHATWG `URL` parser preserves trailing dots in `url.hostname` (e.g., `new URL('http://metadata.google.internal./computeMetadata/v1/').hostname === 'metadata.google.internal.'` and `new URL('http://localhost.:5678/').hostname === 'localhost.'`), exact-match checks against `CLOUD_METADATA` and `LOCALHOST_PATTERNS` are bypassed.

### Root Cause Analysis
1. **Trailing-Dot FQDN Bypass of `CLOUD_METADATA` and `LOCALHOST_PATTERNS` (`src/utils/ssrf-protection.ts:129-139, 349-359, 645-652`)**:
   - `CLOUD_METADATA` (`lines 58-74`) and `LOCALHOST_PATTERNS` (`lines 80-89`) are `Set<string>` collections of bare hostnames (`'metadata.google.internal'`, `'metadata'`, `'instance-data'`, `'localhost'`, `'localhost.localdomain'`).
   - Neither `validateWebhookUrl()` (`line 349`) nor `validateUrlSync()` (`line 645`) strips trailing root dots (`.replace(/\.+$/, '')`) before calling `isCloudMetadata(hostname)` or `isLoopbackHost(hostname)`.
   - Consequently:
     - `SSRFProtection.validateUrlSync('http://metadata.google.internal./computeMetadata/v1/')`, `SSRFProtection.validateUrlSync('http://localhost.:5678/')`, and `SSRFProtection.validateUrlSync('http://localhost.localdomain.:5678/')` return `{ valid: true }` even in **`strict` mode** (incomplete remediation of `GHSA-4ggg-h7ph-26qr` / `CVE-2026-39974` and `GHSA-56c3-vfp2-5qqj` / `CVE-2026-42449` for downstream consumers using `validateUrlSync`).
     - In `validateWebhookUrl()`, `http://metadata.google.internal./` bypasses Step 2 (`isCloudMetadata(hostname)`), permitting GCP metadata access in `permissive` mode.
2. **RFC 6761 `*.localhost` Subdomain Bypass in `isLoopbackHost` (`src/utils/ssrf-protection.ts:137-139`)**:
   - `isLoopbackHost(host)` only checks `LOCALHOST_PATTERNS.has(host)` (`'localhost'`, `'localhost.localdomain'`), omitting `host.endsWith('.localhost')`.
   - Under RFC 6761 (`systemd-resolved`, `nss-myhostname`, macOS, Windows, and Docker), all `*.localhost` names (e.g., `http://admin.localhost:5678/`) resolve locally to `127.0.0.1` / `::1`, yet `validateUrlSync` returns `{ valid: true }` in `strict` mode.

### Impact
- Bypass of `CLOUD_METADATA` and `LOCALHOST_PATTERNS` hostname blocks in `validateUrlSync` (across `strict`, `moderate`, and `permissive` modes) and `isCloudMetadata` in `validateWebhookUrl`.

### Recommended Remediation
Normalize hostnames by stripping trailing dots in both `validateWebhookUrl` and `validateUrlSync`, and treat `*.localhost` and `*.localhost.localdomain` as loopback hosts in `isLoopbackHost`:
