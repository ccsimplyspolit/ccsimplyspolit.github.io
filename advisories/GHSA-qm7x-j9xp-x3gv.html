SSRF Protection Hostname Blocklist Bypass via Trailing-Dot & *.localhost Subdomains (czlonkowski/n8n-mcp)

PUBLISHED GHSA: GHSA-qm7x-j9xp-x3gv LOW (3.1) CWE-918 CWE-184 Published: 2026-10-28

0x01 // Advisory Summary

FQDN trailing dots (localhost.) and RFC 6761 *.localhost subdomains bypass string-equality hostname blocklist checks in URLValidator.validateWebhookUrl() in n8n-mcp <= v2.99.1.
