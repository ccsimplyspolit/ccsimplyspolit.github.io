## Summary

During a defensive static source audit of `https://github.com/cloudflare/mcp-server-cloudflare` (`main` branch, `packages/mcp-common/src/workers-oauth-utils.ts` blob `85c0f096e9`), I identified two input validation defects in the OAuth 2.1 consent workflow:

1. **Unvalidated URL Scheme on Client-Supplied `consent.clientUri` in `renderApprovalDialog()` (`CWE-79`, `packages/mcp-common/src/workers-oauth-utils.ts:62, 281`)**:
   - `renderApprovalDialog()` passes client-supplied `consent.clientUri` through `sanitizeHtml()` (`line 62`) and interpolates it into `<a href="${clientUri}" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">${clientUri}</a>` (`line 281`) without validating that its URL scheme is `http:` or `https:`.
   - Because `sanitizeHtml()` only escapes HTML metacharacters (`&`, `<`, `>`, `"`, `'`), a dynamically registered OAuth client with `client_uri: "javascript:..."` renders an executable `javascript:` link on the `/oauth/authorize` consent origin.
2. **Fail-Open Consent Decision Parsing in `parseRedirectApproval()` (`CWE-20` / `CWE-863`, `packages/mcp-common/src/workers-oauth-utils.ts:336`)**:
   - Line 336 evaluates `decision: formData.get('decision') === 'deny' ? 'deny' : 'approve'`, defaulting any missing or malformed `decision` parameter to `'approve'` instead of requiring an explicit `'approve'` or `'deny'` value.

Public Verification & Patch Advisory: `https://ccsimplyspolit.github.io/advisories/CVD-2026-CLOUDFLARE-MCP-001.html`
